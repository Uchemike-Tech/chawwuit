## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the sensory warmth of open kitchens, artisanal culinary craft, and rapid food e-commerce delivery. The brand personality is vivid, appetizing, approachable, and meticulously organized. It balances high-energy culinary delight with clear, frictionless e-commerce mechanics.

The aesthetic blends **Modern Tactile Warmth** with **Contemporary Editorial Commerce**:
- Clean layouts anchored by crisp product photography.
- Warm, appetizing surface tones replacing sterile corporate grays.
- Generous, rounded touch targets that invite exploration and easy ordering on mobile devices.
- High-contrast visual hierarchy designed to guide the eye directly toward dish presentation, dietary badges, ingredient transparency, and the primary ordering funnel.

## Colors

The palette stimulates appetite and drives purchase momentum through warm, food-inspired chromatic roles:

- **Primary (`#E04E26` - Terracotta Spice)**: Core interactive color for primary order CTAs, cart counters, active states, and dominant brand anchors. Communicates warmth, grilled char, and kitchen energy.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B` - Saffron Honey)**: Used for loyalty rewards, chef specials, golden hour delivery alerts, and promotional ribbons.
- **Tertiary (`#16A34A` - Basil Green)**: Dedicated to dietary trust markers (Vegan, Organic, Farm-to-Table, Halal), checkout confirmations, and live order status rings.
- **Alert / Spice (`#DC2626` - Chili Crimson)**: High-urgency notifications, heat/spice level indicators, sold-out alerts, and critical validation warnings.
- **Neutral Dark (`#1F1916` - Espresso Bean)**: High-contrast typography, icons, and grounded structural elements. Avoid pure `#000000` to prevent harsh visual clipping against warm backgrounds.
- **Neutral Surface & Background (`#FDFBF7` Cream, `#FFFFFF` Surface White, `#E8E3DD` Warm Stone)**: Clean, non-sterile canvas that makes food photography pop while maintaining a warm culinary atmosphere.

## Typography

The type scale uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** across display, body, and UI labels to provide a unified, friendly, and geometric personality with open letterforms.

- Headings use heavier weights (700 and 800) with slight negative tracking to give hero menu items and featured restaurant headers a robust, authoritative editorial feel.
- Pricing tokens (`price-lg`, `price-md`) leverage tabular-friendly, extra-bold figures to ensure instant scanability during basket reviews and menu navigation.
- Label tokens (`label-sm`) use capitalized or upper-tracking styling for dietary indicators, preparation time tags, and nutritional micro-copy.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around an 8pt baseline rhythm using a responsive 12-column fluid grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a single- or two-column staggered layout on mobile screens:

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns, `margin` of `2rem`, `gutter` of `1.25rem`. Food items are organized in 3- or 4-column card matrices with fixed sticky sidebars for cart drawers and filters.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8 columns, `margin` of `1.5rem`, `gutter` of `1rem`. Menu grids sit at 2 columns wide.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns or single-column stack, `margin-mobile` of `1rem`, `gutter-mobile` of `0.75rem`. Cards shift to horizontal list rows or full-bleed cards with sticky bottom-checkout bars.

Spacing scale application:
- `space-xs` (4px): Dietary icon-to-label spacing, badge interior padding.
- `space-sm` (8px): Gap between title and short item descriptions, ingredient tag groupings.
- `space-md` (16px): Internal padding inside food cards, button horizontal padding.
- `space-lg` (24px): Card module separation, section header spacing.
- `space-xl` (40px): Major storefront category block division and banner separation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages warm ambient shadows and tonal surface layering to present dishes tangibly, avoiding sterile gray shadows:

- **Surface Level 0 (Canvas)**: `#FDFBF7` Warm Cream. Base layer for catalog browsing and long-form menu feeds.
- **Surface Level 1 (Card Rest)**: `#FFFFFF` Pure White with a subtle warm border (`1px solid #E8E3DD`) and a delicate warm ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(31, 25, 22, 0.05)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Card Hover / Dropdown)**: Elevated white card with increased warm diffusion: `box-shadow: 0 12px 28px -4px rgba(224, 78, 38, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(31, 25, 22, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Sticky Header / Modals / Flying Cart)**: Floated surface with high elevation: `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -8px rgba(31, 25, 22, 0.12)`. Headers use a frosted cream blur (`background: rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.85); backdrop-filter: blur(12px); border-bottom: 1px solid #E8E3DD`).

## Shapes

The design system uses a `roundedness` level of **2** (Base `0.5rem` / 8px). 

- Interactive containers, food cards, checkout inputs, and image wrappers apply `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) or `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) to retain an inviting, organic softness.
- Dietary tags, status indicators, and compact action pills explicitly utilize full-radius boundaries (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for friendly tactile affordance.
- Overlaid product badges and cart step indicators maintain pill geometries to contrast sharply against squared card frame perimeters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Add to Bag", "Proceed to Checkout")**: Solid Terracotta Red-Orange (`#E04E26`), high-contrast white text, `rounded-xl` (16px), 12px 24px padding. Hover: `#D9381E` with subtle transform scaling (`scale: 1.02`). Active: `scale: 0.98`.
- **Secondary Action ("Customize", "View Details")**: Crisp white background, `1.5px` border in `#E8E3DD`, Espresso typography (`#1F1916`). Hover: `#F8F6F0` surface tint.
- **Quick-Add Icon Button**: 40x40px circle or squircle (`rounded-lg`), Terracotta accent background, high-contrast white plus icon positioned on the bottom right of dish imagery.

### Dietary & Filter Chips
- Rendered in pill shape (`rounded-full`) with a height of 28px to 32px.
- **Vegan / Halal / Fresh**: Light basil tint background (`rgba(22, 163, 74, 0.10)`), `#16A34A` text, subtle leaf or check iconography.
- **Spicy / Extra Heat**: Light chili tint background (`rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.10)`), `#DC2626` text, accompanied by chili pepper icons.
- **Chef's Choice / Special**: Light saffron tint background (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`), `#D97706` text.
- **Interactive Filter Chip**: Unselected state has a `#FFFFFF` fill with a `#E8E3DD` border. Selected state transitions to solid `#1F1916` fill with white text.

### Food Product Cards
- Container: `#FFFFFF` fill, `rounded-xl` (20px), subtle border (`1px solid #E8E3DD`), overflow hidden.
- Media Section: 4:3 or 1:1 aspect ratio food photography, soft zoom-on-hover effect. Overlaid top-left pill chips for tags (e.g., "Popular", "Spicy").
- Content Section: Dish title in `headline-sm`, 2-line truncated ingredient description in `body-sm` (`#6B635B`), price token (`price-md`) pinned to bottom left, quick-add button pinned to bottom right.

### Input Fields & Steppers
- Input Fields: `#FFFFFF` background, `1.5px` border in `#E8E3DD`, `rounded-lg` (12px), 12px 16px padding. Focused state receives a `#E04E26` border and a `0 0 0 3px rgba(224, 78, 38, 0.15)` glow ring.
- Quantity Stepper: Pill-shaped grouped control (`rounded-full`) with an off-white background (`#F8F6F0`). Minus, quantity count, and Plus buttons arranged horizontally with tactile click feedback.

### Sticky Delivery Address Bar & Promo Banners
- Top Address Header: Displays active delivery address, estimated transit time (e.g., "25-35 min"), and delivery/pickup toggle. Features bold Espresso micro-copy and clean arrow dropdowns.
- Promotional Banners: Warm saffron-to-terracotta gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #F59E0B 0%, #E04E26 100%)`) with cream-colored display text, emphasizing promotional discount codes and chef collaborations.
